Privacy Policy

BloodRide Bangladesh

Effective Date: August 1, 2026

At BloodRide Bangladesh, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect the information you provide when registering as a blood donor.

1. Information We Collect

When you register with BloodRide Bangladesh, we may collect the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Date of Birth
  • Gender
  • Blood Group
  • Mobile Number
  • Email Address (if provided)
  • Residential Address
  • Current Location
  • Occupation
  • Educational Institution (if applicable)
  • Emergency Contact (if provided)
  • Previous Blood Donation History
  • Any additional information you voluntarily provide.

2. Why We Collect Your Information

We collect your information to:

  • Register you as a blood donor.
  • Match you with patients requiring your blood group.
  • Contact you regarding blood donation requests.
  • Coordinate blood donation appointments.
  • Arrange donor transportation when applicable.
  • Maintain accurate donor records.
  • Improve our services and operations.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information will only be used for purposes directly related to blood donation and BloodRide Bangladesh’s operations.

We may contact you through:

  • Phone Call
  • SMS
  • WhatsApp
  • Email

4. Sharing of Information

Your information may be shared only when necessary with:

  • Authorized BloodRide Bangladesh staff.
  • Partner hospitals.
  • Licensed blood banks.
  • Healthcare professionals involved in the donation process.
  • Government authorities when required by law.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Although we strive to keep your information secure, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Update your information.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Request removal of your information from our donor database.
  • Decline any blood donation request.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time.

7. Voluntary Registration

Registering as a blood donor is entirely voluntary.

Submitting this registration form does not create any legal obligation to donate blood. You are free to accept or decline any donation request without providing a reason.

8. Data Retention

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to operate our donor database or comply with applicable legal requirements.

If you request deletion of your information, we will process your request within a reasonable time unless retention is required by law.

9. Children’s Privacy

BloodRide Bangladesh does not knowingly collect personal information from individuals who are not legally eligible to donate blood under the applicable laws and regulations of Bangladesh.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page with a revised Effective Date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to update or remove your information, please contact us.
